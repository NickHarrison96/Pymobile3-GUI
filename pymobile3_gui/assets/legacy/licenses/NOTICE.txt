NOTICE for `pymobile3_gui/assets/legacy/`
========================================

This directory holds **data only**. No source code from Legacy iOS Kit
(GPL-3.0) is present here — see `../PROVENANCE.md` §4 for the full list of what
was deliberately left behind and why.

Contents and their terms
------------------------

`manifests/*.plist`
    Apple `BuildManifest.plist`, extracted verbatim from publicly distributed
    signed IPSWs. Authored by Apple. Redistributed as unmodified device
    metadata so a restore can be validated without re-downloading a
    multi-gigabyte firmware package. Apple firmware and its metadata remain
    Apple's; nothing here grants any Apple license or trademark right.

`patches/*.patch`, `patches/ios8/*.patch`
    bsdiff binary diffs (`BSDIFF40` / `BSDIFF407` container, bzip2-compressed
    blocks), applied with `bspatch`. The format is published by Colin Percival
    and carries no per-patch license of its own. The diffs patch Apple firmware
    images; the patched result is derived from Apple firmware and is used only
    on the user's own device, offline, at the user's own election.

    Provenance of the diffs themselves is recorded in `../PROVENANCE.md` §2–3.
    They originate with the SSHRD_Script / Legacy iOS Kit line of work and are
    carried over unrevised.

`PROVENANCE.md`, `README.md`, `NOTICE.txt`
    Written for this project. MIT, same terms as the rest of the repository.

Third-party license texts
-------------------------

None needed yet. When §6.7 (jailbreak payloads) or §6.5 (firmware bundles)
lands and brings in binaries or patches with their own terms, add the
corresponding text here as `<tool>_LICENSE`, matching the naming already used
in `pymobile3_gui/assets/sshrd/licenses/`.

If you add a file here whose license you cannot state, do not add it. That rule
is the only reason this tree is safe to ship.
